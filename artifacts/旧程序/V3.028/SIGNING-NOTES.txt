WHY V3028 EXISTS
----------------
V3026 on target fired its new detector twice while pulling V3027:

  qird_desync have_read=23552 acct=24320 unread=7047 reads=11 total=1
  qird_desync have_read=35072 acct=35840 unread=10341 reads=8 total=2

Both times the reader had accounted for exactly 768 bytes (3 x 256) more than
the modem reported handing over, and both times the surplus was the same 768 --
it did not grow.  Two different things produce that shape:

  * the modem repeating a response, which is real corruption and makes the
    surplus climb read by read; or
  * have_read simply trailing data the modem has already delivered, which is
    harmless and shows up as a surplus that appears once and stays put.

A steady 768 is the signature of the second.  V3026 treated any inequality as
fatal and closed the socket, so it aborted downloads that were very likely fine.
That is a false positive, and it is fixed here rather than left in place.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE V3026/V3027
------------------------------
The per-read AT+QIRD=<ch>,0 check is kept, but the verdict is now split:

  * have_read > acct -- the modem handed over more than the reader ever saw, so
    bytes are gone and the position is unrecoverable.  Socket closed, download
    resumes from the last committed offset with a Range request.  Logged as
    "qird_desync=lost".
  * have_read < acct by a bounded, stable amount -- treated as reporting lag.
    Every change in the surplus is logged as
    "[4G-RX] ch=<n> qird_lag=<bytes> have_read=.. acct=.. unread=.. reads=..",
    and the download continues.
  * surplus above EC800M_QIRD_LAG_LIMIT (4 x 256 = 1024 bytes) -- more than a
    reporting lag can explain, so treated as repetition.  Logged as
    "qird_desync=surplus", same recovery as above.

The qird_lag line is the measurement that settles which of the two mechanisms is
real: a lag that appears once and holds is benign, a lag that steps up read after
read until it trips the limit is repetition.

Known limitation: recovery rolls back only to the last committed 4 KiB
checkpoint, so a genuine repetition detected late can leave already-committed
bad bytes in place and the image digest will still fail.  If "qird_desync=surplus"
appears in a run, that deeper rollback is the next thing to build.

Retained: init commands in state_machine_init() case 1..5 check their
at_send_wait result and retry once, logging
"[4G] init cmd failed step=<n> <cmd>".

WHAT THE DEVICE ACTUALLY VERIFIES
--------------------------------
The FOTA platform re-signs every uploaded package with its own private key
(fota.security.signing-private-key, CryptoService.signPackage) and serves that
signature to the device.  The signature supplied at upload time is not what the
device checks.  The device's trust anchor (include/trusted_public_key.h,
TRUSTED_KEY_LABEL "FOTA-PLATFORM-C0") is the platform's public key, so only the
platform can produce a signature this firmware accepts.
